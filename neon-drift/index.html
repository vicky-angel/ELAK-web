BACKGROUND STORY

The forest used to be quiet. Kale kept one ring of old trees, a patch of grass, and a path home. Then the deer came, and night came with it. Rabbits leapt the ring. Hamsters tore the soil. The trees still stand, but they will not hold without a knight.

Kale is not finished. Each day of practice forges a skill point: a faster step, a tougher heart, a sprout companion, a better gun. Spend those points before the next stand. Five waves. When the fifth begins, dusk drops and the deer charges.

Hold the clearing, and the woods keep their knight.
