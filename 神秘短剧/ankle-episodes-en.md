# The Fake Sprain · 5 episodes

English shot script for Kling. One shot is one 4–6 second clip. Add dialogue as voiceover and subtitles in the edit. Do not let the model render words on screen.

Upload the character stills in `角色参考` as subjects. The whole film is clay-and-paper kale. No humans.

Vertical 9:16. One camera move per shot.

---

## Locked look

Style: clay-and-paper mascots, the same family as the kale rehab coach. The body is one whole leaf. Thin stick arms end in round hands. Thin legs. Small shoes. Rooms are cream and pale wood, warm and simple. Not photoreal. No humans. No subtitles. No text in frame.

Kale (lead, curly kale, file `卡乐.jpg`): olive-green ruffled leaf, cream veins, a small beige bandage sticker on the upper right of the leaf, short black dash eyes and mouth, beige shoes. A white wrap on the right ankle. Arrogant. Unwilling.

Laci (therapist, lacinato kale, file `拉奇.jpg`): long narrow dark blue-green leaf, blistered surface, one thick cream midrib, two short horizontal dash eyes, a flat mouth. No sticker. No glasses. Calm. Almost never smiles.

Russ (assistant, red Russian kale, file `小俄.jpg`): gray-green oak-shaped leaf, purple veins, purple stems for arms and legs, round glasses, both hands holding a blank black tablet. A little nervous.

Redbor (rival, purple kale, file `紫卷.jpg`): deep purple tightly curled leaf, pale purple veins, thin gold round glasses, a small smug smile, dark brown shoes.

Nai (elder, baby kale, file `奶叶.jpg`): smaller, paler, soft green frills, one tiny dewdrop, gentle but firm. Appears only on a screen in episode 5.

Ward: cream walls, a low off-white bed, pale wood floor, one round window, a pale wood chair beside the bed.

Gym: pale wood floor, one large round mirror, a wooden barre, a wooden chair within reach.

Hall: pale wood floor, cream walls, a round window at the far end.
