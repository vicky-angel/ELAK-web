# Character backstories

All five are leaves. The company is Leaf Group. Kale's ankle is fake. The stem is real. Only Nai knows that, and she has told only Laci.

---

## Kale

Curly kale. Current head of Leaf Group. Olive green, with a large ruffled edge that looks impressive in meetings. The white wrap on his right ankle was put on by Russ that morning. It is new enough to shine. The small bandage sticker on the upper right of his leaf is his own. He put it there so he would look injured.

He grew fast, opened wide, and took the chair young. After Nai handed it to him, he proved he deserved it by staying in meetings. The south plot is up for a vote, and Redbor is already counting. Kale does not want to lose that vote face to face, so he calls in sick. The public version is a sprained ankle and three quiet months.

The ankle is fake. The stem is real. The midrib has been quietly stiffening. After long standing and late nights, it feels heavy. He refuses to call that an illness. If he admits it, the chair looks like Redbor's. So he can still negotiate, still hold his leaf high, and still believe he is winning.

He wants to skip the vote, and he wants no one to write his tiredness down as a diagnosis.

## Laci

Lacinato kale. Therapist. The leaf is long and narrow, blistered, with one thick cream midrib. He does not smile, because nothing he does requires an expression.

He takes one kind of patient: the injury in the mouth does not match the injury on the body. Nai paid him to enter the clinic. The terms were short. Finish the sessions. Do not mention the stem yet. Wait until Kale says, out loud, that he is tired. The fee is already paid, so five times the rate, a black card, and a fruit basket do nothing.

He does not care who runs Leaf Group, and he is not on Redbor's side. He counts. The toes point down and pause. The heels stay on the floor. The leaf does not help. Kale thinks he is unreasonable. He was hired to be unreasonable.

He wants the fake movements to become real ones, and he wants the stem kept secret until Kale admits it himself.

## Russ

Red Russian kale. Assistant. A gray-green oak leaf, purple stems, round glasses, and a tablet he is always holding.

He stays with Kale because Kale talks fast and decides fast, and Russ remembers everything that gets forgotten. He bought the wrap that morning. On the tablet is a cancellation list he has shown to no one: night meetings that were moved, afternoons when Kale said he was fine. He has noted the months in which the stem went stiff.

He is loyal, and when he panics he tells the truth. "The doctor said it is basically nothing" and "a small earthquake" both slip out that way. He is not trying to expose Kale. He is afraid Kale will keep answering an old injury with a new wrap.

He wants Kale to finish the sessions, so he can stop covering for a fake sprain.

## Redbor

Purple curly kale. Deputy. The leaf is tight and dark, with gold glasses and a very small smile.

He and Kale came from the same tray of seedlings. Kale unfurled first and got the chair first. Redbor grows slowly and curls tightly. He thinks Leaf Group is loose now because Kale is too good at looking fine. He wants the south plot to pass while Kale is absent. The visit, the fruit basket, and the screenshot are evidence for the board, not a hobby.

He can see that the wrap is too new. He can see that heel raises and one-leg standing do not belong to a crushed ankle. He does not know about the stem. To him, Kale is only a rival hiding from a vote. So he waits, smiles, and says he will come back tomorrow.

He wants the vote, and a picture that proves Kale is faking.

## Nai

Baby kale. The previous head of Leaf Group. She looks small and pale, with soft edges and one dewdrop. Her voice is quiet. Her accounts are not.

She gave Kale the chair. After she stepped back, she watched him prove he did not need rest by adding more meetings. She saw the stem change before he did: the midrib stiffening, while he was still saying he could stand. She did not say it to his face. Kale performs for her.

So she paid for Laci herself and sent him into the clinic. She gives the diagnosis to Laci alone, on the phone. The ankle is fake. The stem is real. Let Kale think he is still winning. Finish the training first. Then treat the stem he refuses to call an illness.

She wants Kale to finish the fake part, so the real part has somewhere to start.
